elite-comms 0.2.0

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.
Drag elite-comms.app to Applications, then open it.
The app runs as a menu bar item with a floating side panel.

Install the Codex CLI separately and sign in with your own account using codex login.
Open the gear tab and enter your country, committee and topic.
Add your position paper and study guides as PDF, Markdown or text files.

First use downloads the speech and voice models. Microphone audio is processed on your Mac.
AI requests send the relevant transcript, your settings and imported brief text to Codex.
This build has a Developer ID signature. Check the GitHub release notes for its notarization status.
